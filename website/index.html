How it works

From your request to proof of delivery, you always know where your goods are.

  1. Request a quote

    Tell us the weight, route and date. We reply within 24 hours.

  2. We assign a truck

    Once you confirm, a driver and the right truck are booked for your load.

  3. Track it live

    You get a tracking link on WhatsApp and can watch the truck on the map.

  4. Proof of delivery

    The driver records who received the goods and when. You see it instantly.
